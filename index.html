About the Model

HIGH FASHION JAISHREE MODEL
Saloni Yogi — Jaishree Model

Graceful Presence, Expressive Detailing & Timeless Poise

Saloni Yogi is a versatile professional model specializing in High Fashion, Traditional Indian and Indo-Western wear, luxury Jewellery campaigns, Beauty & Makeup shoots, and Kurti & Suit wear commercial collections.

With a distinct graceful presence and refined facial expressions, she brings poise and confident styling to every production. Whether carrying intricate bridal lehengas, showcasing the delicate sparkle of traditional heritage jewellery, or delivering high-fashion commercial poses, she maintains subtle elegance suitable for luxury brands and editorial publications.
